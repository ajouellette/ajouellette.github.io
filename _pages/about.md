I am a Research Data Scientist, highly motivated to find solutions to complex and ambiguous problems, often involving large, complex datasets.



At the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, I conducted research in cosmology and astrophysics with the broad goal of deepening our understanding of how [large-scale structure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large-scale_structure_of_the_universe) in the Universe forms and evolves over time. This work has the potential to provide direct insights on the natures of dark matter and dark energy -- two major components of the Universe, about which very little is conclusively known. As part of this work, I used data from a variety of large astronomical surveys ([DES](https://www.darkenergysurvey.org/), [DESI](https://www.desi.lbl.gov/), [SPT](https://pole.uchicago.edu/public/Home.html), and others), large computer simulations of the Universe, and advanced statistical methods (Bayesian statistics, topological data analysis, machine learning, etc.).
